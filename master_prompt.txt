Act as a senior full-stack engineer, data scientist, machine learning engineer, database architect, and professional UI/UX designer.

The application must analyze e-commerce returns, identify products and categories with high return rates, examine the relationship between discounts and returns, investigate delivery delays, compare seller performance, understand customer return behaviour, and generate evidence-based business recommendations.
